# References

External literature, downloaded and kept locally so citations in the wikis can be checked
against the actual papers rather than against memory of them.

**Rule for this directory:** nothing in here is this project's work. Everything is someone
else's, cited by author and DOI, and the wiki pages that lean on it say so explicitly.
Where a claim in the wikis is looser than its source, the wiki gets corrected — not the
source. Downloads are author-hosted PDFs, PMC/Europe PMC open access, or public government
records only.

## `stroke_neuromodulation/`

Post-stroke upper-limb motor recovery: paired vagus nerve stimulation, functional
electrical stimulation, and the Medicare coverage rule that governs access to therapy in
long-term care.

**Start here:** `ASK_THE_DOCTOR_post_stroke_arm_recovery.md` — a preparation document, not
medical advice.

| file | what it is |
|---|---|

| `CMS_Jimmo_factsheet.pdf` | CMS fact sheet, Jimmo v. Sebelius settlement. No "Improvement Standard." |
| `CMS_Jimmo_program_manual.pdf` | CMS program manual clarifications, same settlement. |

Cited but not held locally:

- Dawson J et al. **VNS paired with rehabilitation for upper limb motor function after
  ischaemic stroke (VNS-REHAB): a randomised, blinded, pivotal, device trial.** *Lancet*

**Scope boundary, deliberate:** no spinal-cord-injury material, no passive transport across
injury, no epidural stimulation. Stroke and SCI are different literatures with different
indications and must not be blended in a document that may reach a clinician.
