============================================================

FORWARD ENTROPY — THE DIGIT CEILING

The forward entropy counts maximum information storable at
temperature T. Using the Bekenstein bound:

    S_forward(T) / k_B = 2*pi * E * r / (hbar * c)

the digit ceiling is:

    D_max(T) = floor(log_10(S_forward(T) / k_B))

D_max is the number of decimal digits of the entropy.
It grows sublogarithmically in T and saturates at T_Planck.

INVERSE ENTROPY — THE DIGIT FLOOR

The inverse entropy counts how many digits are needed to
specify T relative to the Planck temperature:

    D_min(T) = ceil(log_10(T / T_Planck))

D_min measures positional specificity on the temperature axis.
It grows as log(T) — logarithmically in T.

Both functions are monotone increasing. They start apart and
must cross exactly once.
