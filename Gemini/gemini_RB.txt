The Concept of Symmetry Breaking

1. The High-Symmetry State (Liquid)

    Translational Symmetry: Continuous (you can move it by 0.0001 mm or 10 miles, and it’s invariant).

    Rotational Symmetry: Continuous (you can turn it by any fraction of a degree).

Once that first atom settles and a second one joins it at a specific distance, the continuous symmetry is broken.

    Translational Symmetry: Becomes discrete. The system now only looks the same if you move by specific intervals (the lattice constant).

    Rotational Symmetry: Becomes discrete. You can only rotate the crystal by specific angles (e.g., 60°, 90°) to make it look the same again.
