## 1. The reading

The author assigns the two fictional characters a role in the mathematics:

| Name | Role in the framework's vocabulary |
|---|---|
| Mr. Burns | the owner who holds the "theorem": the irreducible object, the prime component |
| Waylon Smithers | the executor: the step that couples the object to an effect, "the wreath-product step" |

This is a naming device, an operator pair (a structure and its coupling), in the
same spirit as Red/Blue. It has no derivation behind it beyond the assignment, and
the Krohn–Rhodes / wreath-product vocabulary it borrows is a correspondence, not a
homomorphism ([57](57_boundary_ascending.md) §3).

## 3. "Hack the planet"

The phrase is from the 1995 film *Hackers*. In the framework it is glossed as
"understand what the planet is built on": the claim is that current cryptographic
primitives live in structures below the sedenion boundary, so that two doublings
above them "the complete algebra appears containing both the operation and its
inverse". That claim is `CONJECTURE`:

- no listing in this wiki derives an attack on RSA, ECC, AES or SHA-256 from it;
- the Futurama "two" is confirmed only for permutations ([57](57_boundary_ascending.md) Listing 1);
- the status of the UDEO work and the engine findings that bear on it is in
  [135](135_engine_findings_ledger.md).

The page's own statement, that the aim is comprehension of the structure and not an
attack on people, is the author's, and it matches the author's stated disclosure policy: an embargo applies only
to CVE disclosure, and general findings are shared.
