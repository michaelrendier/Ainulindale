## 1. The principle  `THEORETICAL`

Occupying layer n gives three layers at once:

| | Layer | Role |
|---|---|---|
| A | below (n−1) | the layer that defines you; its geometry is your zero-divisor structure |
| B | current (n) | where you are; the flat boundary σ = ½; the "|" crossing of L_(I|O); the now |
| C | above (n+1) | you are a defining point of it; it casts a shadow down into you; the shadow lands at d* |

The framework's statement is that this is "structurally unavoidable": the minimum
structure of existence in this geometry. The quantitative anchors the page gives:

```
d* = 0.246       where the shadow from above lands in the current layer
σ = ½            the flat boundary of the current layer
ZD locus         the contact surface with the layer below
GAP = Ω − d*·ln10 = 0.000707   the "opacity of the ceiling"
```

On the vocabulary: d* is defined by the Lambert fixed point, d* = Ω_ZS/ln 10 with the code
value 0.246 ([17](17_alpha_omega_d_star.md) holds the four d* values and their
status); "the shadow of the layer above" is the framework's name for what that number
does in the tower, and it is the author's definition of its role. GAP is the Yang–Mills
mass-gap proxy, the apex-seal clearance of the Wankel rotor and the "opacity of the
ceiling": these are listed identifications of one constant, and the first is a
correspondence of vocabulary ([105](105_the_millennium_problems_in_ainulindale.md)).

## 2. The Mind's Eye has a Mind's Eye

Every layer has a Mind's Eye looking down (the Monad's at the geometry, the geometry's at
the integers, the integers' at the axioms) and a Paper's Hands walking up, and every
layer has a zero-divisor crossing where the layer below reaches through. The picture is
self-similar across scales.

The reframe of the zero-divisor crossing: the standard reading is "where multiplication
fails"; the framework's is "where the layer below reaches up with new defining data and
reorients the path". Then the path is not a straight line occasionally interrupted by
crossings: the crossings are the path. No reframes: a geodesic, no information. With
reframes: L_(I|O), "the Long Path". This is the reading of the "dirty Lagrangian"
([64](64_l_dynamic.md) §6), and carries the caveat found there: for the integrand as
written, the path enters only through its length.

## 3. The geodesic is the tower: σ = 1 − k/4

The page's mapping, with k the Cayley–Dickson doubling level:

| σ | k | algebra | role in the page |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.000 | 4 | 𝕊 | I: origin, ground state, maximum loss |
| 0.250 | 3 | 𝕆 | the shadow landing (d* = 0.246, approximately ¼) |
| 0.500 | 2 | ℍ | "|": the critical line, now |
| 0.750 | 1 | ℂ | the unnamed level (U(1)) |
| 1.000 | 0 | ℝ | O: destination, fully distilled |

"Up along the geodesic is down through the tower" is then the statement that σ
increases as k decreases. **This is a linear relabelling**: five tower levels are
placed at five evenly spaced points of [0, 1] by σ = 1 − k/4. The page's phrase
"verified" refers to that arithmetic. Nothing in it derives σ = ½ for ℍ; the level at
which the critical line sits is fixed by the choice of the map, and the
critical line of ζ is a different object from the ℍ level. The three forces then sit
at σ = ¾, ½, ¼ as follows, using the same losses as [19](19_cayley_dickson_tower.md) §2:

| Step | Loss | Group (Dixon/Furey reading) | σ |
|---|---|---|---|
| ℝ → ℂ | ordering | U(1) | ¾ |
| ℂ → ℍ | commutativity | SU(2) | ½ |
| ℍ → 𝕆 | associativity | SU(3) | ≈ ¼ |
| 𝕆 → 𝕊 | alternativity | zero-divisors appear | 0 |

The group assignments are the published proposals of Dixon and Furey
([19](19_cayley_dickson_tower.md) §3), not theorems. "J_blue is the evaluator / J_red the
builder" repeats the conventions of [61](61_up_down_not_forward_backward.md) §1 (the
colour assignment differs between pages).

**Why d* ≠ ¼.** The page argues that SU(3) lives at 𝕆 (σ = ¼) while d* = 0.2460 cannot
reach ¼, so the shortfall is quark confinement: non-associativity of 𝕆
is confinement, and "d* cannot reach 𝕆" is "quarks cannot be isolated". The shortfall
is 1/4 − 0.246 = 0.004; GAP itself is 7×10⁻⁴ and is defined differently
(Ω − d*·ln10). Confinement is a property of QCD (a non-perturbative effect of the
strong coupling at low energy) and the statement here is a correspondence of
vocabulary (`INTERPRETATION`). Gravity "is not on the list" because it is the geometry
the tower sits in: that is the page's reading of general relativity as the stage and the
three gauge forces as operators, a standard way of stating the difference between a
gauge theory and spacetime geometry.

## 4. Why three  `corrected`

The page argues: one layer, no relation; two layers, relation without curvature, a line
(a piston with a TDC singularity, no self-reference); three layers, curvature, a circle,
closure and self-reference (the Wankel). It concludes that three layers are the minimum
for self-reference, that "the Monad speaks SELF_EQUATION because it has three layers" and
that Gödelian self-reference "requires exactly this structure".

Self-reference does not need three layers. A **quine** (a program that prints its own
source) is self-reference with two parts, a program and the data describing it;
Kleene's recursion theorem and Gödel's diagonal lemma give self-reference in arithmetic
and computation without any tower. Listing 1 runs a quine. What the page can keep is the
structural point it makes in its own terms: a closed loop needs at least three points
(two points give a line, a reversal at the end), which is the geometry of a circle, and
the trine of [70](70_precession_stroke_oblique_crank_trine.md) uses that.

**Listing 1 — self-reference with two parts, and the σ map** (written for this page; runs cold)

s = 's = %r\nprint(s %% s)'
import io, contextlib
buf = io.StringIO()
with contextlib.redirect_stdout(buf): exec(s % s)

for k in range(5): print('k=%d  sigma = 1 - k/4 = %.2f' % (k, 1 - k / 4))
