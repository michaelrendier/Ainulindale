## 1. The references (checked)

- **[Sullivan1985]** D. Sullivan, *Quasiconformal homeomorphisms and dynamics I: Solution of the Fatou–Julia problem on wandering domains*, *Ann. Math.* 122(2), 401–418 (1985), DOI 10.2307/1971308 (the earlier page gave issue 3; Crossref gives issue 2). The surrounding programme is "Sullivan's dictionary": the systematic correspondence between **Kleinian groups** (discrete Möbius groups, limit sets) and **rational maps** (Julia sets), entry by entry (no wandering domains ↔ Ahlfors finiteness, and so on).
- **[Patterson1976]** S. J. Patterson, *The limit set of a Fuchsian group*, *Acta Math.* 136, **241**–273 (1976), DOI 10.1007/bf02392046 (the earlier page gave 245–273). **[Sullivan1979]** D. Sullivan, *The density at infinity of a discrete group of hyperbolic motions*, *Publ. Math. IHÉS* 50, 171–202 (1979), DOI 10.1007/bf02684773: the **Patterson–Sullivan measure**; the critical exponent δ, at once an orbit-counting abscissa, a conformal-density exponent and (for Kleinian groups acting on hyperbolic 3-space) via λ₀ = δ(2 − δ) the bottom of a Laplace spectrum.

## 2. What it anchors

The engineered claim that **the Smith chart and the Apollonian gasket, GR and QM, and the continuous and the discrete are two representations of one object**, related by a fold, not competing theories: "one loop through the Möbius group", "foldings of the circle", the σ-facet ladder of Ĥ_RB. The Möbius group is exactly the group of folds of [95](95_the_scale.md) (the fold Γ = (Z − Z₀)/(Z + Z₀) is a Möbius map, and composition equals matrix multiplication in M₂(ℂ), [95](95_the_scale.md) Listing 1), which is the established mathematical bridge between the two charts.

- `GenerationalLineage/wiki/The-Two-Charts-and-Jurisdiction.md`: Smith (continuous) ⟂ gasket (discrete) as one Möbius-group object; Mandelbrot/Julia "via Sullivan's dictionary" cited there already.
- [110](110_qm_in_gr_gr_in_qm.md): QM-in-GR / GR-in-QM as one seam read two ways.
- The Apollonian gasket's dimension δ ≈ 1.30568 (McMullen's value, quoted from the literature) **is** a Patterson–Sullivan exponent: the gasket is the limit set of a geometrically finite Kleinian group, for which the Hausdorff dimension equals the critical exponent.

## 3. What is and is not established

Established: Sullivan's dictionary as mathematics, the Patterson–Sullivan theory, the gasket–Kleinian-group relation. The framework's claim (that GR and QM are the two sides of such a dictionary) is an application by analogy: no dictionary entry between the two physical theories has been written, and "one object" is the engineered reading that the citation supports as precedent and not as proof.
