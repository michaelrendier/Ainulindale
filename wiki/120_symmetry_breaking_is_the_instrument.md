## 1. The one statement, and decoherence

> **To measure X you must instantiate not-X.** The disordered background is not noise contaminating the instrument; it is the other half of the instrument. Symmetry breaking is what makes the figure and the ground at the same time, and neither exists before it.

A measurement is a contrast; no contrast without a reference that is not the thing measured, and the maximally uninformative reference, the one that biases nothing, is the chaotic one. Quantum measurement makes the point without metaphor: a pointer state exists as a classical record only because the system is coupled to an environment with a large number of chaotic degrees of freedom (Zurek's einselection and quantum Darwinism); the environment must be disordered enough to imprint the outcome **redundantly** across many fragments, and that redundancy is objectivity. Landauer closes it: the irreversible, entropy-producing step is the measuring, not a side effect. "The chaos is
not in the way. The chaos is the page." (`ESTABLISHED` as a summary of the decoherence literature; the stronger "symmetry breaking is what makes figure and ground" is the framework's.)

## 6. Inflation is a fit, not a derivation  `ESTABLISHED` (as a description of standing)

Cosmological inflation is a parametrised scalar-field model whose potential V(φ) is chosen to reproduce four observations: flatness, horizon-scale uniformity, a near-scale-invariant Gaussian perturbation spectrum, absence of relic monopoles.
