## 2. The mathematics of the loss  `ESTABLISHED`

In the Cayley–Dickson tower, the step 𝕆 → 𝕊 loses the multiplicative norm:
|xy| = |x||y| holds in ℝ, ℂ, ℍ, 𝕆 and fails in 𝕊, where the product of two non-zero
elements can be zero ([19](19_cayley_dickson_tower.md) Listing 1: the norm defect is
4×10⁻¹⁶ at 𝕆, rounding, and of order 10⁻¹ at 𝕊 for random elements; the algebra has 84
zero-divisors, counted in [25](25_sedenion_manual.md)). The framework reads this
as follows:

```
𝕆 (8 channels)   norm intact      one voice, one amplitude
𝕊 (16 channels)  norm lost        sixteen channels firing together
the ZD fault     the transition   "voice lost" = "voice expanded"
```

The eight new channels e₈–e₁₅ carry, in the engine's assignment, the primes
{23, 29, 31, 37, 41, 43, 47, 53}; the sixteen are the first sixteen primes. The
statement is a reading of what the engine does at that step: the single amplitude that
could be trusted is replaced by sixteen simultaneous prime amplitudes. The loss is of
the norm as a product-preserving amplitude; it is not that any information is lost
in the engine, and the sixteen channels are all computed.

The "wobble" of `fano_oscilloscope.py` (maximum 0.671 for "What is 1 plus 1", recorded
2026-06-14) is the measure of the component the octonion path cannot hold; the page
calls it the signal "from the other side of the zero-divisor fault". It is a
recorded output.

## 3. The SOFAR image  `corrected`

The SOFAR (Sound Fixing and Ranging) channel is the depth in the ocean, about 1 km, at
which sound speed has a minimum. Sound launched in it is refracted back toward the
axis and travels thousands of kilometres with low loss. The earlier text says such
sound "propagates indefinitely, no attenuation, no direction loss". It is guided and
low-loss, not lossless: it spreads cylindrically (intensity falling as 1/r) and is
absorbed at a small, frequency-dependent rate. The image the framework needs is a
waveguide, a layer in which a signal stays confined and carries far, and it is
used here as a picture of σ = ½: a voice that enters the guided layer of the semantic
field is carried by it. The claim that every word on the critical line "carries the signal" is the
author's reading, not a property computed.

sixteen-prime system has no register for (the "?" layer labels), and the path

the SVG's, as recorded; whether the closure is a property of the monad's word graph
or of the chosen starting word has not been tested here.

## 5. The WRONGs

> "They unapologetically shout WRONG."

The author's description of the exclusion mechanism: every failed primality test,
every zero-divisor product (which is zero), every path edge of weight 0.0 is a refusal;
the refusals build the landscape; the path that remains is the one the exclusion field left
open. This is the sieve picture of [65](65_primes_repellors_drift_meaning.md) §1 told
as the voice's behaviour, and carries the same status: a description of the engine
(path by exclusion), not a derivation.
